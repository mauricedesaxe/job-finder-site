## Video direction

Use Job Fiend's pale canvas, navy type, sky blue accents, and DM Sans. The approved 3D mascot is the focal image in each shot. Motion follows the words, then settles for a readable hold. Keep the 3D images sharp and their original colors. Do not add invented product screens, results, statistics, or claims. Leave enough space around the mascot for the headline and CTA.

## Frame 1 — Keep your spark

- scene: The walking Job Fiend introduces the promise
- duration: 6s
- poster: 4.6s

- narrativeRole: hook

The site promises a private search run with the job seeker. The headline keeps that value in the viewer's own terms.

Scene 1 (0.0–1.5s): The pale canvas and a small “A private search, run with you” label appear. The walking mascot enters from the right and settles in a large image plane. Asymmetric 55/45 layout with the text side still open.

Scene 2 (1.5–3.5s): “Find the jobs.” arrives on the left in a clean text cascade. “Keep your spark.” follows beneath it and becomes the dominant line. The mascot makes one small, bounded walking step rather than looping. Use `waterfall-entry` for the words and `spring-pop-entrance` for the mascot.

Scene 3 (3.5–6.0s): The promise holds while a slim sky blue rule draws beneath it. Motion settles by 4.5s so the viewer can read the full line. Keep the mascot and label visible through the cut.

## Frame 2 — Start together

- scene: A daily search begins with a setup call
- duration: 6s
- poster: 4.8s
- transition_in: cut
- status: animated
- src: compositions/frames/02-setup.html
- narrativeRole: cta
- blueprint: compose
- asset_candidates: assets/job-fiend-call-3d.webp — approved booking mascot, focal

The site says the search runs each day, and the first jobs are reviewed together on a setup call. Invite that first step without promising a placement.

Scene 1 (0.0–1.7s): The booking mascot enters from the left against the same pale canvas. A navy header on the right reads “A search that starts with a conversation.” Asymmetric 45/55 layout, with the image large enough to recognize the phone.

Scene 2 (1.7–3.9s): Two short lines appear one after the other beneath the header: “I run the search for you.” and “We look at the first jobs together.” Each line arrives as its own pill card, leaving the setup invitation clear. Use `spring-pop-entrance` for the cards.

Scene 3 (3.9–6.0s): “Book your setup call” enters in a solid navy pill with a small arrow. The cards hold and the mascot settles. Leave the CTA readable for the last 1.5s.
